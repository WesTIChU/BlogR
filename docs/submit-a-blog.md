# ► Submit a Blog

**Know a brilliant little blog that deserves more attention?** Send it our way.

BlogR is all about discovering **smaller, independent websites**, personal writing, niche interests, and people who share things simply because they enjoy it.

We're particularly interested in **lesser-known blogs** that might otherwise go unnoticed. You don't need thousands of readers, a fancy website, or a team behind you. Just something interesting to share.

<hr class="blogr-intro-divider" aria-hidden="true" />

## What we're looking for

- **Independent blogs** and personal websites.
- **Original, human-written content** with a genuine voice.
- **Niche interests**, unusual subjects, and personal experiences.
- **AI-built websites are welcome**, provided the content is written by humans.

## What we don't accept

- Large commercial publishers or established media outlets.
- Blogs with their main content hidden behind a **paywall**.
- **AI-generated articles**, content farms, or mass-produced writing.
- Websites primarily focused on **selling products, services, or courses**.
- Promotional websites with little original content.

A few adverts, affiliate links, or a donation button are absolutely fine. **The writing just needs to come first.**

Every submission is reviewed manually, and **inclusion isn't guaranteed**.

<hr class="blogr-intro-divider" aria-hidden="true" />

## Before submitting

**Please search the BlogR Directory first** to check whether the website is already listed.

We appreciate every suggestion, but duplicate submissions won't be considered.

<hr class="blogr-intro-divider" aria-hidden="true" />

## Submit via GitHub

**Have a GitHub account?** This is the preferred way to submit a blog.

[**► Open the GitHub Issue Form**](https://github.com/WesTIChU/BlogR/issues/new?template=submit-blog.yml)

The form asks for the blog's name and URL, with optional fields for a description, category, and additional information.

<hr class="blogr-intro-divider" aria-hidden="true" />

## Submit via Email

**No GitHub account? No problem.**

Send your suggestion to:

**add [at] blogr [dot] directory**

Replace `[at]` with `@` and `[dot]` with `.`.

Please include the **blog name**, **website URL**, and a **short description** explaining what makes it worth including.

Unsolicited marketing, bulk submissions, and promotional emails will be ignored.
