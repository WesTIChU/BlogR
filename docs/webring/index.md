# ► Join the BlogR Webring

**Help people find the good corners of the independent web.** The BlogR
Webring is a free way for independent websites to introduce visitors to one
another through a small, friendly navigation widget.

## What is a webring?

A webring is a group of websites connected by shared links. Instead of ending
with one page, a visitor can follow the ring to discover another site, then
another one after that. It is a simple, human-sized alternative to relying
only on search engines and social feeds.

The BlogR Webring is for sites listed in the BlogR Directory. Joining is free,
and there is no advertising or membership fee.

## How the Webring works

Each member receives a permanent Webring ID and a small three-link widget:

<div class="webring-example" role="img" aria-label="Example Webring navigation: Previous, Random, Next">
  <span>Previous</span><span aria-hidden="true"> · </span><span>Random</span><span aria-hidden="true"> · </span><span>Next</span>
</div>

- **Previous** takes visitors to the previous verified site in the ring.
- **Random** takes visitors to another verified site.
- **Next** takes visitors to the next verified site in the ring.

Only verified websites participate in Webring navigation. A site that is not
currently verified will not be offered as a destination.

## Who can join?

The Webring is open to independent blogs, personal websites and other
independent web projects that are listed in the BlogR Directory. You should:

- control the website where the widget will be installed;
- keep the three Webring links visible in the public HTML;
- use the permanent Webring ID assigned to your site; and
- have the widget available on one of the approved pages: `/`, `/blog`,
  `/links`, `/website` or `/webring`.

If your site is not listed yet, [submit it to BlogR](/submit/) first. Inclusion
in the directory and Webring participation are reviewed separately.

## How to install the widget

After your site has a Webring membership, use the widget supplied for its
assigned ID. Place the three links on your home page, blog, links page,
website page or Webring page. Keep them visible to visitors and do not change
the assigned ID or the navigation destinations.

This page is intentionally informational while public Webring controls are
being prepared. No disabled Webring endpoint or interactive installation
control is exposed here.

## Automatic verification

BlogR checks the approved pages automatically in the background. Verification
looks for all three links and confirms that they use the correct permanent ID.
Once you've installed the widget, BlogR will automatically check your website.
New memberships are prioritised and are normally checked within 5–15 minutes.
Pending failures retry after 15 minutes with increasing delays, while verified
members are rechecked every 24 hours.

The widget must remain installed for your website to stay verified. If the
links are removed or no longer match the assigned widget, BlogR retries the
check automatically. Previously verified sites have a 48-hour failure grace
period before they are suspended; sites that have never verified remain
pending until they pass a check.

After installing the widget, the **Request verification check** control in the
widget popup can place a bounded recheck request in the queue. It does not run
an immediate fetch, and repeated requests are rate-limited and de-duplicated.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does joining cost anything?

No. Joining the BlogR Webring is free.

### Do I need to add a BlogR badge?

No. The Webring widget is separate from the optional BlogR Directory badge.

### Will my site be included immediately?

No. Your site needs a BlogR Webring membership, the widget needs to be
installed, and the automatic verification check needs to find all three links.

### What happens if I remove the widget?

Your site will no longer be verified and will not be included in Webring
navigation. You can install the widget again and wait for the next check.

### Can I move the widget later?

Yes. Move it between the approved pages, but keep the complete widget visible
in the page's publicly returned HTML.

### Is the public member list available now?

Not yet. The background verification system is being tested privately first.
This information page is available for review without enabling public Webring
navigation.
