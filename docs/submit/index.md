# ► Submit a Site

<div class="blogr-submission-notice" role="note" aria-label="Submission guidelines">

### 🛡️ Important: Please read before submitting

Every website submitted to BlogR is **manually reviewed and moderated** before being added to the directory. **Submission does not guarantee inclusion.**

**AI-assisted website design, coding and structure are absolutely welcome.** However, articles, stories and other main editorial content should be **original and human-written**, not filled with mass-produced AI-generated content.

We want to showcase **genuine voices, creativity and the independent web**.

</div>

**Know a brilliant independent site, blog or community that deserves more attention?** Send it our way.

BlogR is all about discovering **smaller, independent websites**, personal writing, niche interests, online communities, and people who share things simply because they enjoy it.

We're particularly interested in **lesser-known blogs, independent websites and online communities** that might otherwise go unnoticed. You don't need thousands of readers, a fancy website, or a team behind you. Just something interesting to share.

<hr class="blogr-intro-divider" aria-hidden="true" />

## What we're looking for

- **Independent blogs** and personal websites.
- **Independent Web projects**, tools, archives, fansites and webrings.
- **Online communities** and discussion spaces.
- **Original, human-written content** with a genuine voice.
- **Niche interests**, unusual subjects, and personal experiences.
- **AI-built websites are welcome**, provided the content is written by humans.

## What we don't accept

- Large commercial publishers or established media outlets.
- Sites with their main content hidden behind a **paywall**.
- **AI-generated articles**, content farms, or mass-produced writing.
- Websites primarily focused on **selling products, services, or courses**.
- Promotional websites with little original content.

A few adverts, affiliate links, or a donation button are absolutely fine. **The writing just needs to come first.**

Every submission is reviewed manually, and **inclusion isn't guaranteed**.

<hr class="blogr-intro-divider" aria-hidden="true" />

## Before submitting

**Please search the BlogR Directory first** to check whether the website is already listed.

We appreciate every suggestion, but duplicate submissions won't be considered.

## Submit via Email

**No GitHub account? No problem.**

Send your suggestion to:

**add [at] blogr [dot] directory**

Replace `[at]` with `@` and `[dot]` with `.`.

Please include the **site or community name**, **website URL**, and a **short description** explaining what makes it worth including.

Unsolicited marketing, bulk submissions, and promotional emails will be ignored.

## Listed on BlogR? Link back to us!

If your blog, website or community is listed on BlogR, we'd love a wee link back!

You can display our badge anywhere on your website. It's a simple way to help others discover independent sites and communities.

**It's completely optional.** You don't need to add a badge or link to be included in the directory.

### Copy the HTML

Copy the code below and paste it into your website wherever you'd like the badge to appear.

```html
<a href="https://blogr.directory" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
  <img
    src="https://blogr.directory/share-blogr-image.png"
    alt="Listed on BlogR Directory"
    width="250"
    style="max-width: 100%; height: auto;"
  >
</a>
```

**Want a smaller or larger badge?**

Simply change `width="250"` in the code.

For example:

- `width="150"` for a small badge.
- `width="200"` for a medium badge.
- `width="250"` for the default badge.
- `width="300"` for a larger badge.

The height adjusts automatically, so the image keeps its proportions.

The badge is hosted by BlogR, so there's no need to download or upload the image yourself.
