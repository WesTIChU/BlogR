# ► Hosting

Hosting services for independent blogs and personal websites.

<hr class="blogr-intro-divider" aria-hidden="true" />



## Beginner-Friendly Hosting

* **[Bluehost](https://www.bluehost.com)** - Popular starter hosting, especially for WordPress.
* **[SiteGround](https://www.siteground.com)** - Reliable hosting with good performance and support.
* **[Hostinger](https://www.hostinger.com)** - Affordable hosting with a simple interface.
* **[DreamHost](https://www.dreamhost.com)** - Long-standing provider with solid features.

## Managed Hosting

* **[WP Engine](https://wpengine.com)** - Premium managed WordPress hosting.
* **[Kinsta](https://kinsta.com)** - Fast, modern hosting built on Google Cloud.
* **[Flywheel](https://flywheelwp.com)** - Simple managed WordPress hosting.

## VPS & Cloud Servers

* **[Hetzner](https://www.hetzner.com)** - Powerful, affordable VPS and dedicated servers.
* **[DigitalOcean](https://www.digitalocean.com)** - Developer-friendly VPS with simple pricing.
* **[Linode](https://www.linode.com)** - Reliable cloud hosting with good performance.
* **[Vultr](https://www.vultr.com)** - Flexible VPS with global locations.
* **[RackNerd](https://www.racknerd.com)** - Budget VPS deals.
* **[HostHatch](https://www.hosthatch.com)** - High-storage VPS with good value plans.
* **[BuyVM](https://buyvm.net)** - Cheap VPS with a strong community reputation.
* **[GreenCloud VPS](https://greencloudvps.com)** - Affordable VPS with solid performance options.
* **[Time4VPS](https://www.time4vps.com)** - Budget European VPS hosting.
* **[LowEndBox](https://lowendbox.com)** - Deals and listings for cheap VPS providers.

## Modern & Edge Hosting

* **[Vercel](https://vercel.com)** - Ideal for frontend frameworks and static sites.
* **[Netlify](https://www.netlify.com)** - Easy deployment for static and JAMstack sites.
* **[Cloudflare Pages](https://pages.cloudflare.com)** - Fast global hosting with built-in CDN.
* **[Render](https://render.com)** - Modern platform for web apps and services.
