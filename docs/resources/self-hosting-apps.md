# ► Self-Hosting Apps

Applications people use to run their own websites and services.

<hr class="blogr-intro-divider" aria-hidden="true" />



## Communities & Forums

* **[Discourse](https://www.discourse.org)** - Modern forum software used by many large communities.
* **[Flarum](https://flarum.org)** - Lightweight and clean forum platform.
* **[NodeBB](https://nodebb.org)** - Real-time forum powered by Node.js.

## Comments & Interaction

* **[Isso](https://isso-comments.de)** - Self-hosted alternative to Disqus.
* **[Utterances](https://utteranc.es)** - GitHub-based comment system.
* **[Giscus](https://giscus.app)** - Modern comment system powered by GitHub Discussions.

## Analytics (Privacy-Friendly)

* **[Plausible](https://plausible.io)** - Simple, privacy-focused analytics.
* **[Umami](https://umami.is)** - Lightweight alternative to Google Analytics.
* **[Matomo](https://matomo.org)** - Full-featured analytics platform.

## Monitoring & Uptime

* **[Uptime Kuma](https://github.com/louislam/uptime-kuma)** - Monitor your website uptime with a clean dashboard.
* **[Healthchecks](https://healthchecks.io)** - Monitor cron jobs and background tasks.
* **[Cachet](https://cachethq.io)** - Status page system for your services.

## Newsletters & Email

* **[Listmonk](https://listmonk.app)** - Self-hosted newsletter and mailing list manager.
* **[Mailtrain](https://www.mailtrain.org)** - Email marketing platform you host yourself.

## Automation & Integrations

* **[n8n](https://n8n.io)** - Automate workflows between your apps and services.
* **[Activepieces](https://www.activepieces.com)** - Open-source automation platform.
